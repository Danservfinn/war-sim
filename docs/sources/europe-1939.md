# Europe 1939 collection

The date is 1 September 1939. The opened page says: "On September 1, 1939, at 4:45 a.m. (local time), units of Germany's armed forces attacked Poland." [1] Footnote 2 of that page was not opened.

- Adolf Hitler, Führer und Reichskanzler, 2 August 1934 to 30 April 1945. [2]
- George VI, King, 11 December 1936 to 6 February 1952. The style on this date includes Emperor of India, through 22 June 1948. [4]
- Arthur Neville Chamberlain, Prime Minister and First Lord of the Treasury, 28 May 1937 to 10 May 1940. [3]
- Albert Lebrun, Président de la République française, 10 May 1932 to 11 July 1940. [6]

- Ignacy Mościcki, President of the Republic, 4 June 1926 to 18 September 1939. He ceased to exercise the office on leaving Poland that day, and resigned on 30 September 1939. [7]

The League Armaments Year-Book, Official No. C.228.M.155.1939.IX, Geneva, June 1940, was opened. [43] The preface says it shows peace establishments on the eve of September 1939, and that it does not contain mobilisable forces or reserve war material. Germany: the book says the official documents it obtained include no data on effectives. The 900,000 figure is labeled unofficial and estimated, end of 1938. Not copied. France: the table is budgetary effectives, and the columns do not stay with a year. The Law of 20 March 1939 fixes land-army officers for 1939 at 31,060. That is a legal ceiling, not a count of men on 1 September. Not copied.

The U.S. Strategic Bombing Survey report on the German war economy was opened from the HathiTrust full view, Michigan copy `mdp.39015017684799`. [46] The Archive copy is still HTTP 401. The text is OCR, not the PDF. Table 25 states construction employment in prewar Germany on 17 May 1939. That is a headcount, not a plant rate and not a count of men under arms. Not copied. The appendix district tables are 1943-44, and the columns do not stay with the district. Not copied.

Table 10 states the strength of the armed forces of the United Kingdom at the end of September 1939, in thousands: total 1,270, Royal Navy 180, Army 897, Royal Air Force 193. That is the end of the month, not 1 September. Copied as a strength count. Not a formation. Not an order of battle. Not a plant. The stock cells on that table are end of period. The 1939 stock is end-1939. Not copied. The page does not say whether the ton is long or metric. Not converted.
